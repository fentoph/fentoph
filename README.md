# Fentoph

> **Building technology with purpose.**

Fentoph is an independent, founder-led technology company focused on building modern software, digital products, and practical technology solutions.

## About Fentoph

Fentoph exists to turn ideas into useful technology. We build and explore products across software, web platforms, APIs, automation, cloud systems, and other areas of modern technology.

Our approach is simple: understand the problem, design a thoughtful solution, engineer it well, and keep improving it.

### What we focus on

- Software engineering
- Web applications and platforms
- Mobile and digital products
- APIs and backend systems
- Cloud infrastructure
- Automation and developer tooling
- Product design and experimentation

## About the CEO

### Founder & CEO

**Fentoph's founder and CEO** leads the company's vision, product direction, engineering, and strategy.

As a founder-led company, Fentoph currently combines leadership and hands-on technical work. The CEO is directly involved in product development, architecture, technical decisions, experimentation, and building the company's long-term foundation.

The goal is to transform promising ideas into reliable products while establishing Fentoph as a lasting technology company.

## Vision

To build a technology company that creates useful, reliable, and thoughtfully designed products with long-term value.

## Mission

**Build useful technology. Solve real problems. Keep improving.**

## Principles

**Simplicity** — Build clear solutions without unnecessary complexity.

**Quality** — Favor reliable, maintainable, and well-engineered systems.

**Innovation** — Explore better ideas, technologies, and ways of working.

**Design** — Treat user experience as an essential part of the product.

**Ownership** — Take responsibility for what we build and how we build it.

**Long-term thinking** — Build foundations that can grow with the company.
